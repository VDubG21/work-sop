Refunds and delivery

Applies to orders placed on our own website. Our TOS does not offer refunds, so every refund is an exception we choose to make. The operator on shift decides.

Zero tolerance for scammers. Whenever you verify an account, also decide whether the customer is trying to get a free game. If so, we never issue a refund, whatever the customer says or threatens.

Always ask for the Order ID. No refund and no replacement without one.

Signs of a scammer

  • On our own site, the customer says the account doesn't work and asks for a refund, but in fact they changed the email themselves.
  • The customer implies that someone else bought it for them, but cannot give the order ID. This is an instant suspect.

These are the patterns we know so far. When you identify a scammer, there is no refund and no replacement. Ban them in Warehouse and add them to the matching group, with the reason, and block them in PrestaShop (the Blacklist group)*.

* The PrestaShop block is the Blacklist group, which is not set up yet. Until it is, the Warehouse ban and group is what works: it tells every operator about the customer.

The account was delivered

1
Explain, don't refundTell the customer that under our TOS we can't refund. Explain that it works the same as buying a key, and that the accounts are new. Most customers understand and agree.
2
If they keep insisting, verify the accountCheck whether it was used, whether we can recover it, and whether the customer is trying to get a free game.
3
Not used and recoverable: make an exceptionTell the customer we are making an exception, then issue the refund. You decide this yourself.
4
Used, or not recoverable: no refundThe answer stays no, unless the customer meets one of the angry customer triggers below.

The angry customer exception

If the account was used and we would normally say no, we still refund when the customer puts us at risk of a dispute. The operator decides, then follows the blocking steps below. This never applies to a customer you have identified as a scammer. Manager can change

Refund if the customer does any of these:

  • Says they will open a dispute or a chargeback with their bank or payment provider.
  • Threatens to report us to G2A, the payment provider or a consumer authority, or threatens legal action.
  • Threatens a negative review or a public post about us.

This list is a guide, not a checklist. Customers won't always say it in these words, so use your judgment on what is a real threat and stay flexible with the customer.

Insults, swearing, caps lock, impatience or simply insisting are not triggers on their own. Insults follow the abusive customers chapter, and the answer on the refund stays the same.

The account was not delivered

Customer is aggressive

Refund immediately, no questions asked.

Customer is calm

Explain that the product was advertised as a backorder and delivery can take up to 24 hours. They may choose to wait. If they still want a refund, we refund them.

Dead on arrival or wrong region

Only if reported within 2 hours of delivery Manager can change

Check the account very carefully in the internal audit panel, then decide: is it really dead or in the wrong region, or is the customer trying to scam us? If it is confirmed dead, replace it immediately. Refund only as a last resort (see the replacement ladder). If you decide it is a scam attempt, there is no replacement and we never refund. After 2 hours it is no longer dead on arrival: the order is treated like any other delivered account, and the warranty rules apply (see Replacements).

When we refund to avoid a dispute

If the customer used the account and we still refund to avoid a dispute, ban them in Warehouse and add them to the matching group, with the reason, and block them in PrestaShop (the Blacklist group)*. The operator decides on the spot, and the manager does not need to be told.

* The PrestaShop block is the Blacklist group, which is not set up yet. Until it is, the Warehouse ban and group is what works: it tells every operator about the customer.

Blocking, banning and groups

These are three separate actions in three tools. None of them obliges you to do the others.

Live Chat: ban

Stops an abusive customer from chatting with us (see Abusive customers). A PrestaShop block is not mandatory after it.

Warehouse: ban and group

Kept for our own statistics, and it is also how every operator learns about the customer. Always add the customer to the matching group, with the reason, so the next agent who meets them knows why. The groups already exist in Warehouse, and you will know which one to pick. A PrestaShop block is not mandatory after it.

PrestaShop: block

The block is the Blacklist group, which is meant to stop the customer from buying from us. Write the reason, date and operator in the customer note. The Blacklist group is not set up yet, so for now the Warehouse ban and group is what works.

The manager reviews the banned customers list once a week.

Resellers

Resellers are not allowed. If we catch one:

  1. Ask them not to purchase from us anymore.
  2. Force refund their order.
  3. Tell them that they will be blacklisted and that all their upcoming orders will be automatically declined, to discourage them from buying again.
  4. Ban them in Warehouse and add them to the reseller group.
  5. Block them in PrestaShop (the Blacklist group)*, which is what will decline their upcoming orders automatically.

How to recognize a reseller:

  • They ask us directly for an offer, which most resellers do.
  • They buy several copies of the same game.
  • They want to use the accounts in their game center, which is also not allowed.

* The PrestaShop block is the Blacklist group, which is not set up yet. Until it is, the Warehouse ban and group is what works: it tells every operator about the customer.

Customers who threaten a dispute, a chargeback or a bad review

Disputes and chargebacks are handled outside Live Chat, by a dedicated operator. In the chat, your job is to stay polite and leave a clean record, because the dedicated operator takes the proof from the conversation. These cases will happen, and some will end in a dispute whatever you do.

Stay calm and polite

Don't argue and don't answer pressure with pressure. The chat history is evidence and will be read.

Promise and admit nothing

Don't promise any result about the dispute and don't admit fault. Stick to the rules in this chapter.

Check the angry customer exception

If the customer meets a trigger, refund and block as described above.

Credentials revealed, viewed or accessed

This is our main defense in a dispute. The order is not refundable by default, including "I thought it was a key, not an account" claims, because the disclosures on the site and the marketplace state clearly that it is an account.

You do not need to report or hand off disputes. The dedicated operator checks them directly on the payment processor's site.

Replacements

Our products have a lifetime warranty, and the warranty means a replacement. A refund is the last resort.

Verify before you replace

Within 3 months of delivery the account should still work exactly as it was sent, and in practice almost all of them do. After 3 months a replacement may really be needed. In both cases the operator checks the account first and decides which of these it is:

  • Still working: no replacement.
  • Working, but the customer changed the email, or otherwise manipulated the account: the warranty is void, because we can no longer verify that it is the account we sold. No replacement, and no refund. If you find this clearly, the answer is final. Changing the password alone does not void the warranty. On G2A orders an email change is judged case by case (see G2A orders).
  • Not working from our side: replacement.

Zero tolerance for scammers. If the customer is trying to get a free game, there is no replacement and we never refund. Then ban them in Warehouse, add them to the matching group and block them in PrestaShop, as described in Refunds and delivery.

Opening a ticket

  1. Ask for the Order ID. A replacement is never issued without one.
  2. Create the ticket in Warehouse. This is mandatory. You can also create it in the Live Chat app, but never only there.
  3. Use the Purchase verification tool to start the replacement, or to register a task when the game is out of stock.
  4. Warehouse notifies us when the game comes into stock, and we deliver.

When a replacement can't be delivered

We promise a replacement within 24 to 48 hours Manager can change, counted from the moment the ticket is created in Warehouse. If we can't make it, work down this ladder:

1
Same gameKeep trying to source it.
2
Keep the customer informedTalk to them when they come back. Most customers wait.
3
Offer a different gameAfter 5 days of waiting, the customer can pick another game priced at what they paid, within 10% either way. We do not replace an old game with a brand new release, even if the customer paid the same amount. If the customer asks for a new release, the operator asks the manager what to do. Manager can change The operator decides.
4
Refund, only if the order is under 3 months oldManager can change The operator decides. After 3 months a refund is practically not possible: the customer keeps waiting for the same game, or takes a different one.

There is no fixed limit on replacements for a real customer. In practice a customer may get a replacement after 3 months, and another one about 3 months after that. This is not a hard rule. The operator checks the account every time the customer contacts us, and requests that come much more often than that are a sign to look at the account more carefully.

G2A orders

G2A orders keep the warranty even when the buyer changed the credentials.

  • We don't mention the warranty first and we don't rush to replace. Start with assistance.
  • Assistance means giving the customer the 2FA code needed to log in to the account. It does not include a replacement. Manager can change
  • G2A's own message asks buyers to change the credentials. That voids the warranty we give on our site because we can no longer verify the account, but our manager decided to still offer assistance and warranty on G2A orders.
  • Replacements on G2A orders are done by the operator, the same as on our own site, including when the credentials were changed. Before replacing, the operator verifies the account (see "Verify before you replace" in Replacements). The ticket is created in Warehouse, with the Purchase verification tool, exactly as on the website.
  • If the customer changed the email on a G2A order, the warranty is not automatically void. The operator judges it case by case, depending on the customer, and the password alone never counts.
  • G2A has no response deadline, unlike our website. So the 24 to 48 hour promise and the different-game offer from Replacements do not apply here. The customer can wait longer until we have a replacement.
  • Refund on a G2A order: if the account was not revealed, refund, no questions asked. If the account was revealed, we do not refund.
  • We have met scammers on G2A but no resellers yet. We do not ban them for now, it is not necessary yet.

G2A tickets: two places, always. Verify and complete the ticket in the G2A Dashboard as well, not only from the email notification. The Order ID is in the G2A Dashboard. No replacement without it.

Abusive customers

Frustration is not abuse. Find the level, then do what the last column says. There are three levels.

LevelTypeExamplesWhat you do
1TenseFrustration, rude tone, caps lock, sarcasm, swearing about the shop or the situation: "I've waited two days", "this is unacceptable"Not abuse yet. Stay polite and give a clear timeline, then bring the customer back to the subject with a small reminder: we are here to solve the problem, not to listen to complaints. It is a gentle nudge, not a formal warning.
2Personal abuseInsults aimed at you, mocking, spamming, continuing after a reminderSend a boundary message, then one final warning. If it continues, ban the customer in Live Chat. They can continue by email. Manager can change
3SevereSexual comments, slurs, discriminatory language, targeting you by nameSkip the ladder. Ban in Live Chat right away and save the chat as evidence.

Repeated abuse across channels (chat, email, reviews) or aimed at one operator by name is treated as level 3.

Rules at every level

Their order rights don't change

Warranty and refund eligibility never depend on behavior. Only the channel changes, from chat to email.

Abuse is not a reason to refund

Insults do not count as an angry-customer trigger. Refunds follow the rules in chapter 1.

Live Chat ban only

No Warehouse ban and no group for abuse. Those are only for scammers and resellers (chapter 1).

Closing message to use: we can continue by email, and the customer's order rights stay the same. A ready-made macro for the boundary message and the final warning will be added here.
